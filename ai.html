About FiDemonlist

FiDemonlist is a custom Geometry Dash Demonlist. This page provides machine-readable information about the current list and Legacy Demons.

Current demons are ranked from #1 downward. Legacy Demons are former FiDemonlist demons that have fallen off the current list.
